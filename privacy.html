Privacy policy

Hoppalo Studio · last updated 17 October 2026

Hoppalo Studio (“the app”) is a private production tool used only by the owner of the Hoppalo YouTube channel. This page explains what the app does with data it gets from Google.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • Managing the channel's videos (scope youtube.force-ssl): to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the channel and to add videos to its playlists.
  • The channel's analytics (scope yt-analytics.readonly): the channel's own views, watch time, average view duration and audience retention.

It does not read comments, subscribers' identities, messages or any other personal information, and it does not access any channel other than the one it was connected to.

How the data is used and stored

  • Analytics numbers are used only to show the owner how the channel's videos perform and to plan new videos.
  • Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer: the sign-in token and a copy of the channel's numbers. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and none is used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The app does not use cookies or tracking.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on Google's Third-party apps & services page, or with “Disconnect” in the app, which deletes the stored sign-in token. The stored copy of the analytics is deleted along with the app's data folder, or within 30 days on request.
