Hoppalo Studio

The production tool behind the Hoppalo YouTube channel: happy songs, stories and learning videos for little ones.

Hoppalo Studio is a private app used only by the channel's owner. It makes the channel's animated videos and, with the owner's permission, uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload finished videos, their thumbnails, captions and Shorts to the Hoppalo channel, and add them to the channel's playlists;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention) to decide which videos to make next.

It is not offered to the public and no other accounts use it.
